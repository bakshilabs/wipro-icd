Deal origination

How deals arise in each move

My plan has two moves: expand Wipro's existing UKI accounts first (Move 1), then convert that proof into new logos (Move 2). Deals reach the pipeline by different routes in each, which changes how they score at bid/no-bid. The shares are my estimate, to test against Wipro's CRM data.

Move 1: expand

Existing accounts, through the client partner who owns the account.

  • Proactive proposals from white-space maps and executive workshops, raised with the client partner60%
  • Early renewal or add-on to a run contract, with QE, modernisation or AI scope30%
  • Reactive RFP or re-compete inside the account10%

In the scorecard these deals usually score higher on economic buyer and champion, because the client partner gives access and the live service gives a sponsor.

Move 2: new logos

New clients, hunted from the first quarter.

  • Proactive, opened by a dated forcing event (operational resilience, ISO 20022, DORA)30%
  • Proactive, sourced with a partner (Tricentis, AWS, Google, Anthropic)20%
  • Reactive RFP from a new client or sourcing adviser30%
  • Public framework call-off or tender20%

These deals usually start lower on relationship and access. The forcing event, the partner and a Move 1 reference are what lift the score before bid effort is committed.

Wipro's CEO ties large-deal success to being "proactive with our clients" and to qualifying the pipeline (Q2 FY25 call). Under the Procurement Act 2023, framework call-offs generally require competition.

    UK public sector

    Procurement Act 2023

    The Act applies to procurements started on or after 24 February 2025. Six provisions change how I would shape and price a public-sector IC&D bid.

    Section 19

    Most advantageous tender

    Authorities award to the most advantageous tender, assessed against published criteria and weightings. The proposal outline follows those weightings section by section.

    Section 20

    Two procedures

    The open procedure is a single stage. The competitive flexible procedure allows negotiation, several stages and down-selection, which gives room to refine scope and price.

    Section 51

    Standstill

    A mandatory standstill of eight working days starts when the contract award notice is published. Mobilisation plans start after it ends.

    Section 52

    Published KPIs

    Contracts above £5m need at least three KPIs set before signature. The three most material are published and assessed at least every 12 months. I would link AI-enabled service designs to KPIs we can measure and price against.

    PPN 017

    AI in tenders

    Central-government buyers may ask whether AI helped write the tender and whether that content was checked, and whether AI is part of the service. The answers are for information and are unscored. Buyers can bar training AI on their confidential data.

    Thresholds

    From 1 January 2026

    The central-government threshold for goods and services is £135,018 including VAT, and the sub-central threshold is £207,720 (law-firm summaries of SI 2025/1200).

    Who funds what

    I would ask each partner what it can fund before the client is asked to pay: proofs of concept, specialist engineers, licences for a pilot or a joint executive briefing. Wipro would fund pursuit effort and solution design. The client funds the paid assessment or first phase. The split is agreed in writing at the bid gate (my approach).

    Partner-sourced pipeline

    I would log partner-sourced and partner-influenced opportunities as separate fields in the CRM, register deals with the partner where its programme allows, and review the list with each partner's UK account team every month. Partner-sourced pipeline then becomes a measure I report alongside my own (my approach).

    One story for the client

    The client should see one proposal with one accountable lead. I would agree with the partner which parts carry its name, who presents what in the orals, and how its references sit next to Wipro's, such as the Lloyds Banking Group Payment Services Platform award for QE.
