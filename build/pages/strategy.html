Strategy

Two moves, run in order and from the first quarter. This page sets out the moves, the three figures behind the order, and the four measures I would be judged on. The detail sits in the Appendix pages linked under each move.

Arpan Bakshi, London. Research date 27 October 2026. Figures from Wipro's Q2 FY27 datasheet, Form 20-F FY2026 and the Q3 FY25 earnings call. Targets are my estimate.

Grow share of wallet in Wipro's existing UK and Ireland accounts first (Move 1: expand), and use that proof to open new ones (Move 2: new logos).

Existing accounts qualify faster, convert at a higher rate and book order book sooner, and the references they produce make hunting credible. Both moves run from the first quarter.

Move 1: expand

Existing accounts, from the first quarter

  1. Map IC&D white space in the top UK and Ireland accounts with the client partners, banking first.
  2. Attach QE, modernisation and AI to run contracts and renewals, where added scope offsets step-downs of 6% to 7% a year.
  3. Run executive workshops on dated forcing events that end in a proactive proposal.
  4. Phase first scopes so order book lands early and the next phase is already named.

Pages behind it

A8Account Map. Named UK and Irish clients, white space and the people behind each account. A7Accounts. Scoring model, offering adjacency and order book build. A10Workshops. Executive formats that end in a proposal. A12Deals. Pricing, renewal step-downs and the margin calculator. A13Banking Pursuit. Land and expand at an illustrative bank.

Move 2: new logos

New accounts, hunting from the first quarter

  1. Take references from Move 1 to targets with a dated trigger: bank integrations, resilience, DORA and PS26/2.
  2. Build partner-sourced pipeline with Google, Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft and Tricentis.
  3. Open with a fixed-price diagnostic, then expand.
  4. Run hunting as a standing motion from the first quarter.

A9New Logos. Targets, dated triggers and routes in. A4Demand. Forcing events that give a reason to call. A6Buyers. The buying committee and its objections. A5Competitors. Positioning against the incumbent. A11Pursuits. Scorecard and stage plan for proactive bids.

Why this order

From Wipro's figures to the two moves

Almost all revenue sits in existing accounts, the large-account count is still below FY24, and the CFO has named hunting as a lever; so expand first, rebuild large accounts, and hunt from the first quarter.

Measures

Four measures

The first two are my own numbers. The third is shared with each client partner, and the fourth is read against a public benchmark.

1 My own number Signed TCV

IC&D order book booked

Signed total contract value for the assigned portfolio, against Wipro's bookings definition. Phased first scopes land it early.

2 My own number 3 to 4×

Pipeline created and coverage

New qualified pipeline each quarter, and coverage against target. Three to four times target is my estimate of the level to hold.

3 Shared measure Per account

Share of wallet

Wipro's share of each client's addressable spend, tracked with the client partner as the lead measure for Move 1.

4 Benchmark 47%

Win rate

Loopio's 2026 benchmark puts the UK RFP average at 47% and the global average at 39%. Proactive expansion work should sit above it.
