First Months

This page sets out how I would spend my first year in three phases: listen, decide and deliver. Banking and financial services accounts come first, with quality engineering and payments resilience as the entry offer. It covers who I meet, how I review accounts and pipeline, my first workshops and pursuits, and how that builds into order book contribution.

Two responsibilities this page answers. "Own contribution to Industry Cloud & Digital Revenue and Order Book (OB) targets for assigned portfolio" and "Expand Wipro's presence across existing and new strategic UKI clients". The plan below works back from both.

First focus

Banking and financial services first

I would start with the banking and financial services accounts in my portfolio and open with quality engineering and payments resilience. The reasons below come from Wipro's results calls and the regulators; the sequence is my approach, to agree with my manager.

Where Europe is growing

In Q2 FY27 Wipro's Europe market unit grew 5.1% year on year in constant currency, with "broad-based growth led by BFSI". In April 2026 management reported "good traction in the U.K., specifically in the BFSI sector".

Wipro Q2 FY27 and Q4 FY26 earnings call transcripts (primary)

Why QE and payments

UK banks face fixed payment dates: CHAPS rejects unstructured addresses from November 2026 and needs purpose codes on all payments from November 2027. FCA PS26/2 incident and third-party reporting applies from 18 March 2027. Each one needs regression and resilience testing.

Bank of England; FCA (primary). Full timeline on Demand

Proof I can cite

Lloyds Banking Group's Payment Services Platform with Wipro won Best Test Automation Project at the European Software Testing Awards 2023. Wipro also has a payment-testing teaming agreement with Iliad Solutions (March 2026) and is a Tricentis Global Platinum Partner with 3,000+ Tosca specialists.

Wipro TESTA 2023 page; Iliad Solutions release; Wipro Tricentis page (primary)

  1. Payments resilience reviewA short paid review of one payment flow against the CHAPS, ISO 20022 and PS26/2 dates, run with the QE practice and Capco.
  2. Automation proof on one releaseTest automation on a live payments or core banking release, with Tricentis or Iliad Solutions where they fit and WEGA with Harness for the pipeline.
  3. Managed QE and resilience testingAn ongoing QE service with resilience evidence the bank can use with its regulators.
  4. Modernisation and AICore, payments-hub and AI work, using Enable the Core or Extend the Core, partners such as Intellect, Finastra or Temenos, and the data readiness step on the AI Engagement page.
