First focus

Banking entry offer

Both moves start in banking and financial services with quality engineering and payments resilience. In Move 1 the offer attaches to a run contract or renewal through the client partner. In Move 2 it opens on a dated forcing event at a bank where Wipro is new.

Why QE and payments

UK banks face fixed payment dates. CHAPS rejects unstructured addresses from November 2026 and needs purpose codes on all payments from November 2027. FCA PS26/2 incident and third-party reporting applies from 18 March 2027. Each one needs regression and resilience testing. In April 2026 Wipro's management reported "good traction in the U.K., specifically in the BFSI sector".

Bank of England; FCA; Wipro Q4 FY26 earnings call (primary). Full timeline on Demand

Lloyds Banking Group's Payment Services Platform with Wipro won Best Test Automation Project at the European Software Testing Awards 2023. Wipro also has a payment-testing teaming agreement with Iliad Solutions (March 2026) and is a Tricentis Global Platinum Partner with 3,000+ Tosca specialists.

Wipro TESTA 2023 page; Iliad Solutions release; Wipro Tricentis page (primary)
