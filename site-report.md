## 1. Purpose and audience

The site supports Arpan Bakshi's application for the ICD Practitioner role in Wipro's Industry Cloud & Digital (IC&D) portfolio in the UK and Ireland (UKI). It is designed to be shown on screen during a panel interview with no set brief, and then shared with Wipro afterwards.

The site does three jobs. It reads Wipro's own public figures to work out what the role has to achieve. It sets out a strategy and a twelve-month plan for achieving it. It then backs every part of that plan with sourced analysis, working models and evidence from Arpan's career.

The work is independent. It draws only on public sources and Arpan's CV, uses no Wipro logo or brand colours, and states in its footer that Wipro did not produce or review it. Every page carries `noindex` so search engines do not list it.

## 3. Reading conventions used on every page

The pages follow a written style guide, and knowing its labels makes the site much easier to read.

- **Sources.** Every page ends with a Sources panel. Each source is marked primary (filings, statutes, regulators, company releases) or press (journalism, analysts, brokers, vendor surveys).
- **"My judgement" and "my estimate".** Ratings, scores, plans and forecasts that are Arpan's view carry one of these labels.
- **"Placeholder figures".** Numbers in the commercial models are stand-ins for Wipro's own data. The models invite the reader to type in real values.
- **"Draft message to test".** Example client messages are drafts to try with account teams. They are not claims about what Wipro says.
- **"To confirm".** A claim with only a single secondary source, or one whose status may have changed, is marked this way.
- **Outside reading.** Statements about Wipro's structure and position are presented as a reading from public sources, with what would confirm them.
- **Named people.** The pages name only Wipro's chief executive, chief financial officer and Europe chief executive, plus Wipro, Capco and Designit leaders cited on the Account Map with their published titles.

Most pages are interactive. Charts respond to selection, and several pages hold working models with sliders and inputs. Important content is visible without hovering.

## 4. The central argument

The whole site rests on one chain of reasoning.

### What Wipro's figures say

Four figures from Wipro's Q2 FY27 results (15 October 2026) and its FY2026 Form 20-F set the brief.

| Figure | Reading |
|---|---|
| 98% of revenue from existing clients in Q2 FY27 | Growth has to come mostly from accounts Wipro already holds |
| Clients above US$100m a year fell from 22 (FY24) to 17 (FY25) and 16 (FY26), then rose to 18 (Q2 FY27) | The large-account base eroded and is being rebuilt |
| Europe grew 5.1% year on year in constant currency, led by BFSI, Consumer and EMR | Europe, 27.4% of IT services revenue, is growing, with banking among the leaders |
| IT services operating margin of 16.5% | About 7.5 points below TCS and 1.7 points below HCLTech, so margin matters on every deal |

Wipro added 33 new clients in Q2 FY27 and 216 in FY26. The CFO, Aparna Iyer, said in January 2025 that "hunting remains a very, very key lever".

### Three asks of the role

From those figures the site derives three things the role has to do:

1. Grow share of wallet in existing accounts, because that is where almost all revenue sits.
2. Open new logos with insight, on a dated reason and a reference, through partners who already hold trust.
3. Hold the margin at the first scope, by setting scope, pyramid and pricing model before a proposal goes in.

### The strategy: two moves

The strategy is to grow share of wallet in Wipro's existing UK and Irish accounts first (**Move 1: expand**) and to use that proof to open new ones (**Move 2: new logos**). Both moves start in the first quarter.

The site gives the reason for the order. Existing accounts qualify faster, convert at a higher rate and book order book sooner, and the references they produce make hunting credible.

**Move 1 has four parts.**
- Map IC&D white space in the top UKI accounts with the client partners, banking first.
- Attach quality engineering (QE), modernisation and AI to run contracts and renewals, where the added scope offsets price step-downs of 6% to 7% a year.
- Run executive workshops on dated forcing events that end in a proactive proposal.
- Phase first scopes so order book lands early and the next phase is already named.

**Move 2 has four parts.**
- Take references from Move 1 to targets with a dated trigger, such as bank integrations, resilience rules, DORA and FCA PS26/2.
- Build partner-sourced pipeline with Google, Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft and Tricentis.
- Open with a fixed-price diagnostic, then expand.
- Run hunting as a standing motion from the first quarter.

### Why banking leads

Banking leads because the public evidence is strongest there and the dates are fixed.

- **Lloyds Banking Group** is the largest Wipro estate named on the public record. Wipro describes a relationship of more than fifteen years, a Wipro speaker at PegaWorld 2025 put it at 18 years and about 2,500 Wipro staff, and Lloyds with Wipro won two European Software Testing Awards in 2023, including one for its Payment Services Platform.
- **Fixed dates.** FCA PS26/2 incident and third-party reporting applies from 18 March 2027. Purpose codes become mandatory on every CHAPS payment in November 2027. The Bank of England deferred the November 2026 CHAPS address change on 27 August 2026 with no new date.
- **New-logo triggers.** Coventry Building Society absorbs The Co-operative Bank on 1 January 2027. Santander and Nationwide have acquisitions to integrate. The Google Cloud London region failure of 20 October 2026 disrupted several UK banks, and regulators owe the Treasury Committee a full reply by 20 January 2027.

If Arpan's assigned portfolio turns out to be another sector, the site shows that the method holds and only the dates change. Energy suppliers must complete half-hourly settlement migration by 7 May 2027. The PSTN copper switch-off falls on 31 January 2027. Insurers face Consumer Duty on closed books and the pensions dashboards backstop. Public bodies publish procurement pipeline notices each May, and Wipro is on G-Cloud 15.

### The plan: twelve months in three phases

| Phase | Move 1: expand | Move 2: new logos | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listen, months 1 to 3 | Meet every client partner; map footprint, sponsors and white space; agree rules of engagement and credit; learn the cost model from India presales and delivery | Build the target list on dated triggers; agree partner plans with alliance teams | Three to five account plans with IC&D plays; one proactive proposition per top account |
| Decide, months 4 to 6 | Run the first executive workshops; shape phased first scopes with Delivery and Finance | First reference-led meetings; first fixed-price diagnostics proposed | Qualified pipeline at three to four times target; first phased scope signed in a banking account |
| Deliver, months 7 to 12 | Book phase ones; qualify phase twos that reach large-deal size; bring renewals forward with added scope | Convert diagnostics into first contracts; each new logo becomes a Move 1 account | Order book from expansions; large deals qualified for year two; pipeline carried forward |

### Four measures

1. **IC&D order book booked**: signed total contract value for the portfolio, against Wipro's bookings definition.
2. **Pipeline created and coverage**: new qualified pipeline each quarter, held at three to four times target.
3. **Share of wallet per account**: the shared lead measure, tracked with each client partner.
4. **Win rate**: read against Loopio's 2026 benchmark, which puts the UK RFP average at 47% and the global average at 39%.

### The vision

The vision is that the UKI IC&D practice becomes the part of Wipro that client executives call before they write a tender. Each top account carries a standing IC&D point of view refreshed against its regulatory calendar. Each dated event becomes a workshop that ends in a proposal. QE and modernisation enter through run contracts and renewals, AI enters through the data and adoption work those programmes expose, and references compound into a new-logo motion.

### A3 AI Engagement

- **Regulation timeline.** From 5 February 2026 the Data (Use and Access) Act replaced Article 22 with a right of challenge. EU chatbot transparency duties applied from 2 August 2026. EU AI Act high-risk duties for credit scoring and insurance pricing apply from 2 December 2027.

- **Adoption programme.** Twelve months in five phases: baseline and design, data readiness, pilot agent assist, scale self-service, extend and optimise. Each phase has a gate, and placeholder measures (containment, CSAT, handling time, digital adoption) show a glide path.

## 10. Points to check before the interview

Reading the pages side by side turned up some points worth settling before they come up in conversation.

- **The Foster + Partners figure appears in two forms.** Most pages say the pitches and bids Arpan led won "more than £10m a year in fees". The Buyers and Workshops pages say the presentations "helped win contracts worth more than £10m". It is worth choosing one form and using it everywhere.
- **Wipro's Anthropic tier is open.** The Competitors page states that Wipro is not an Anthropic Global Premier partner, and the New Logos page says the tier Wipro holds in Anthropic's services track is "to confirm with the alliance team". A panel member may know the answer.
- **Items marked "to confirm".** These include the Swift validation status (2022 guide), several public-sector pipeline values taken from search extracts, current tiers for Temenos, Finastra and Guidewire, and three competitor contract values reported in US dollars.
- **Placeholder figures stay placeholders.** Deal bands, margins, pipeline outputs and the £14m year-one order book are Arpan's estimates. The site invites Wipro's own data to replace them, and it helps to say so when showing them.
- **Overview and meeting pages are hidden.** They are no longer in the navigation, but their text still refers to "the bar at the top of every page" and to M codes. If the meeting pages are shown in the interview, they need to be opened by address, for example `/situation.html` or `/overview.html`.
- **The research date is 27 October 2026.** Any event after that date, such as the 4 November 2026 initial regulator reply to the Treasury Committee, will have moved on by the time of the interview.
