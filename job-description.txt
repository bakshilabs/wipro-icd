ICD Practitioner, Industry Cloud & Digital (IC&D), UK and Ireland
Wipro

Text as supplied by Arpan Bakshi on 27 October 2026.

Role Purpose
The ICD Practitioner role is responsible for driving strategic growth across Wipro's Industry Cloud & Digital portfolio in the UKI, with a particular focus on digital transformation, customer experience transformation, AI-led business innovation, application modernisation, and strategic consulting engagements.

Key Responsibilities
- Own contribution to Industry Cloud & Digital Revenue and Order Book (OB) targets for assigned portfolio.
- Partner with Account Managers, Client Partners, and Pursuit Teams to shape qualified IC&D opportunities.
- Drive hunting and farming motions by identifying transformation opportunities within existing and new accounts.
- Act as Practice Specialist for IC&D pursuits, supporting opportunity qualification and deal closure.
- Position and sell IC&D Practice Offerings (Application and Cloud Modernization/Adoption, APR, Digital Experience & Commerce, Quality Engineering and Testing, Devops/ CI-CD, AI Solutions- Gemini/Claude, AI Adoption /Operating Mainframe, Legacy Modernization -Java/.NET, Digital Applications, Integration across different products /technology stack).
- Understanding of one or more technologies or products above
- Shape commercially viable and margin-positive deals by influencing scope, solution design, and pricing in collaboration with Sales, Delivery, and Finance.
- Support RFPs, proposals, and commercials with a clear focus on margin protection and cost optimization.
- Advise clients on AI-enabled business transformation, customer experience strategy, and technology modernisation.
- Facilitating executive workshops to align business, technology, and customer experience priorities.
- Identify and shape large-scale digital transformation opportunities, end-to-end customer experience strategies aligned to business objectives
- Expand Wipro's presence across existing and new strategic UKI clients.
- Designing AI-enabled customer engagement models and digital service experiences.
- Build executive relationships and act as a trusted advisor to client leadership teams.
- Collaborate with Wipro global practices, delivery organisations, and alliance partners to create differentiated solutions.
- Leading Software Engineering /Digital Experience/ AI transformation programmes that improve customer engagement, digital adoption, and business outcomes.
- Travel within UKI to support customer engagements and pursuits as per pursuit requirements
