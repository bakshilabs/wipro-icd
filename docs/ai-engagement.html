AI Engagement

This page sets out how I would design AI-enabled customer engagement and lead the programmes that deliver it. It covers the published evidence, an engagement model across channels with where Claude and Gemini fit, use-case priorities, rules for human oversight, the regulation timeline and an adoption plan with measures.

Based on NBER research, company and vendor case studies, Gartner releases, Wipro press releases, the FCA, the ICO and law-firm summaries of the EU AI Act. Use-case placements, oversight levels and programme figures are my judgement or placeholders.

Evidence

What AI does in customer service

The strongest evidence is for helping human agents, especially new ones. Automated resolution rates are high in vendor case studies. Klarna's reversal shows the quality risk of cutting people too fast, and Gartner's surveys show most service leaders keep or widen human roles.

      Regulation

      Rules and dates

      UK firms face existing FCA rules, a new automated decision regime and, where they serve EU customers, the EU AI Act. The timeline runs from February 2026 to August 2028. Select a date for detail.

      Regulation timeline, 2026 to 2028

      Three dates fall within the next 14 months: EU marking of AI-generated content, the FCA evaluation and EU high-risk duties.

      FCA

      The FCA relies on existing frameworks: Consumer Duty, SM&CR and operational resilience. Its second AI Live Testing cohort includes Barclays, Experian, Lloyds Banking Group and UBS.

      Automated decisions

      From 5 February 2026 the Data (Use and Access) Act replaced Article 22 with a right of challenge and safeguards. Token human review falls short of meaningful involvement.

      EU AI Act

      Chatbot transparency duties applied from 2 August 2026. Credit scoring and insurance pricing are high-risk, with duties from 2 December 2027. Irish clients and UK firms serving EU customers are in scope.

      ICO

      The ICO consulted on draft automated decision guidance from 31 March to 29 May 2026. The final guidance is to confirm.

      Programme

      Adoption programme

      A twelve-month programme in five phases, following the pattern of DSIT's Scan, Pilot, Scale approach. It starts with agent assist, where the evidence is strongest, and widens self-service once measures hold. Data readiness is its own phase, running alongside design: in October 2026 Wipro's CEO said banking clients are focused on "creating AI-ready data foundation" as well as core and data modernisation (Q2 FY27 earnings call).
