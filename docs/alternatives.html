    Pricing

    How AI is changing services pricing

    Analysts, brokers and the firms themselves describe the same shift. Productivity from AI is passed back to clients at renewal or earlier, and new deals carry productivity commitments from the start.

    2–4%

    AI-driven revenue deflation a year, as acknowledged by companies (DSP market summary, March 2026). TCS reportedly put it at 2–3% in traditional segments.

    24 months

    Clients are reopening IT services contracts within about two years of signing. Contracts above $50m a year face the most pressure (HFS Research, June 2026).

    3–5 years

    New Infosys deals increasingly build in AI productivity commitments at the start over these terms, with repricing pressure greatest at bid and renewal (brokers via Business Today).

    2–2.5 years

    Wipro's average contract tenor, per its CFO on 15 October 2026. Many contracts have already been refreshed, and Wipro expects to reach "that inflection curve in a few quarters".

    +10 pts

    Gross margin of Globant's AI Pods above traditional delivery. Clients pay for output or consumption. AI Pods are about 2% of Globant's revenue.

    Platform

    Wipro's CEO describes AI-native unit platforms as "a combination of platform plus services". Kyndryl brands its agentic modernisation as "services-as-software".

    What I take from this for IC&D pursuits. I would lead with outcome commitments, show the productivity curve openly in the commercial model, and protect margin through Wipro Intelligence platforms and IP. The Deals page models what each lever does to contribution margin.
