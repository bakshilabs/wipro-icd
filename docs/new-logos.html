New Logos

This page sets out how I would hunt new logos for IC&D in the UK and Ireland. Move 2 converts proof from expanding existing accounts into first contracts at new clients, starting with banking, then insurers, utilities and the public sector, with hunting running from the first quarter.

Based on public sources retrieved on 27 October 2026: company results, regulators, Parliament, Find a Tender, the Government Commercial Agency and partner programme pages.     Public sector pipeline

    Pipeline notices and frameworks

    Under the Procurement Act 2023 (section 93), large contracting authorities publish what they plan to buy. That turns public sector hunting into a dated list. Crown Commercial Service became the Government Commercial Agency (GCA) on 1 April 2026, and the frameworks below now sit with the GCA.

    £100mAn authority that expects to pay more than this under relevant contracts in the coming financial year must publish a pipeline notice.
    £2mEach contract over this value that it plans to advertise is listed separately.
    56 daysPublished within 56 days of 1 April, so by 26 May. The next deadline is 26 May 2027.
    18 monthsEach notice covers 18 months from the start of the financial year. Private utilities are encouraged to publish too.

    Central departments also publish rolling commercial pipelines under the Sourcing Playbook, refreshed at least every six months. Source: Welsh Government and Cabinet Office guidance on pipeline notices; GOV.UK Sourcing Playbook (30 Sep 2026).

      Routes in

      Routes to a first meeting

      Buyers spend little time with suppliers and avoid irrelevant outreach, so I would arrive through someone they already trust or with a funded offer. Partner programmes now pay for assessments and proofs of concept, and Wipro holds senior status with most of them.

      Which route I would use first, by sector

      In banking I would lead with a Move 1 reference, then an insight brief on the trigger. Pick a sector.

      Route strength from 1 to 5 is my judgement of how often each route gets a first meeting in that sector.

      17%of B2B buying time is spent meeting potential suppliers, about 5% each when three are considered. Gartner, 750 buyers.
      73%of buyers actively avoid suppliers who send irrelevant outreach; 61% prefer a rep-free experience. Gartner, 25 Jun 2025.
      67%prefer a rep-free experience and 45% used AI in a recent purchase. Gartner, 9 Mar 2026.
      1.8xmore likely to complete a high-quality deal when buyers use supplier digital tools with a sales rep. Gartner.

      Gartner also reports that 99% of B2B purchases are driven by organisational change and that buying groups have 5 to 11 stakeholders. Executive briefing benchmarks from association surveys are older and paywalled, so I use the Gartner figures as the main evidence.
