Banking

Banking leads because the public evidence is strongest there and the dates are fixed. If the portfolio is another sector

The method holds and the dates change

Each sector has a fixed date in the same window and an entry offer of the same shape: a small, dated first scope that puts Wipro inside the change.

Energy

7 May 2027

Suppliers must complete migration to market-wide half-hourly settlement.

Entry offer. A two-week settlement readiness check, then integration testing of settlement, billing and data flows.

Telecoms

31 Jan 2027

The PSTN switch-off, with the hardest customers still to move.

Entry offer. Automation of the outreach and ordering steps for the last customers, with the systems behind them consolidated.

Insurance and pensions

Closed books

Consumer Duty applies to closed books, and the pensions dashboards connection backstop is in the calendar.

Entry offer. A closed-book outcomes review, then AI-assisted complaint handling with a colleague making each decision.

Public sector

Each May

Authorities publish pipeline notices under the Procurement Act, and Wipro is listed on G-Cloud 15.

Entry offer. An insight brief ahead of the pipeline notice, with a discovery scope bought through G-Cloud 15.
