Working with practices and partners

How I would run a joint pursuit

A combined proposition needs the market unit, several practices and at least one partner to agree one story. This is the sequence I would use, based on how I co-created and sold a new advisory service line at Foster + Partners.

  1. Agree the account view with the client partnerStart from the client's dated pressures on the Demand page and the client partner's knowledge of the account. Agree which two or three outcomes the proposition is for.
  2. Bring the practices into one discoveryInvite the IC&D practice specialist, Designit for experience work, Capco for financial services business cases and the AI-Native unit's forward-deployed engineers where agents are in scope.
  3. Give the partner a named roleAsk the Google, Anthropic, Microsoft or package partner's UK account team what they can fund or bring, such as a proof of concept, engineers or a joint executive briefing.
  4. Test the message in an executive workshopUse the draft message above as a starting point and change it after the client's leaders have reacted to it.
  5. Shape scope and price with delivery and financeAgree which platform gains, such as WEGA's lab-stated velocity figures, go into the estimate and how much of that benefit is shared with the client in price.

Building and selling a service line

At Foster + Partners I co-created and launched a strategic advisory service line covering corporate environmental strategy, digital transformation and spatial consulting. I led pitches and bids that won more than £10m a year in fees, and presented to Fortune 500 chief executives.

Earlier, at SOM in New York and at WSP, I ran business development that brought in more than $3m and more than £2m a year through outreach, pitches and bids.

Selling Claude, Gemini and Copilot work

As co-founder of Atlas Learn I train executive teams to use AI tools. I have trained more than 250 senior professionals on Claude Code, Cowork and other tools, and delivered board-level training for a FinTech.

I hold Anthropic credentials in AI Fluency, Claude Code in Action, Claude Cowork and the Claude Platform, and a Microsoft Copilot Studio credential. I use Claude Code, Gemini, NotebookLM, Python and C# in my own work.
