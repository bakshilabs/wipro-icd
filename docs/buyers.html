Buyers

This page sets out who buys an IC&D programme in the UK and Ireland, what makes each executive act and what they object to. It ends with how I would build a relationship with each of them over the first four quarters.

Triggers come from dated regulatory and market events in my research, each with its source. Influence scores, objections and the relationship plan are my judgement. Replies are labelled as drafts to test with Wipro's account teams.

      Relationships

      Relationship plan

      Each executive gets a sequence across the first four quarters: a first meeting with something useful to them, a working session, evidence from a live pilot and a review. I would agree each step with the Wipro client partner who owns the account. How I reach each executive depends on the move: expanding an existing account (Move 1) or opening a new logo (Move 2).

      Experience

      My work with executive buyers

      The plan rests on three parts of my work: presenting to chief executives, training boards to use AI and selling advisory services to senior clients.

      Fortune 500

      Chief executive presentations

      At Foster + Partners my presentations to Fortune 500 chief executives helped the design studios win contracts worth more than £10m. Clients included Roche, Genentech, Alibaba, Apple, Oracle, Hyundai and JPMorgan Chase.

      At SOM in New York I presented to executives at major Wall Street banks and won more than $3m a year in fees.

      250+

      Board-level AI training

      At Atlas Learn I designed and delivered AI training for the board of directors of a FinTech company, and for another client's chief executive and its heads of HR, marketing and IT.

      I have trained more than 250 senior professionals to use Claude Code, Cowork and other AI tools.

      £10m+ a year

      Advisory selling

      At Foster + Partners I co-created and launched a strategic advisory service line covering corporate environmental strategy, digital transformation and spatial consulting.

      I led pitches and bids that won more than £10m a year in fees, including a multi-year contract with a global pharmaceutical company.

      How it carries over. I would open every first meeting in the plan above with a short brief on the dated pressures facing that client, drawn from the evidence on the Demand page.
