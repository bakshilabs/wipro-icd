First Months

This page sets out how I would spend my first year in three phases: listen, decide and deliver. It covers who I meet, how I review accounts and pipeline, my first workshops and pursuits, and how that builds into order book contribution.

Every target and figure on this page is my estimate, to set with my manager. Context from Wipro's quarterly results and the research behind this site.

Two responsibilities this page answers. "Own contribution to Industry Cloud & Digital Revenue and Order Book (OB) targets for assigned portfolio" and "Expand Wipro's presence across existing and new strategic UKI clients". The plan below works back from both.
