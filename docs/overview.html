Reading notes

How to read this site

Sources on every page

Each page ends with a Sources panel. Every entry gives the publisher and date and is marked primary or press. Claims from a single secondary source say so.

Judgement is labelled

Scores, ratings and plans that are my view carry a label such as "My estimate" or "my judgement". Example messages are marked as drafts to test.

Placeholders are labelled

Figures in the commercial models are placeholders, marked as such. Wipro's own rate cards, pipeline and deal data would replace them.

Models are editable

Sliders, weights and inputs change the scorecards, calculators and order book builds as you use them. Charts respond to selection, and their main reading is written under each title.
