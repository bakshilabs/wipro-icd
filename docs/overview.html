Overview

Situation

What the figures ask of the role

Wipro describes its strategy as consulting-led and AI-powered. In the UK and Ireland that strategy meets a mature book: growth has to come from inside the accounts Wipro already holds, new logos have to be opened with a point of view, and every deal has to hold its margin. The job description asks for two things that rarely sit in one person, advising client leadership on AI-enabled transformation and shaping margin-positive deals with Sales, Delivery and Finance. On screen: M1 Situation.

  • Grow share of wallet in existing accounts. With 98% of revenue from existing clients and the large-account count still below FY24, expansion is the fastest route to order book.
  • Open new logos with insight. The CFO has said "hunting remains a very, very key lever". New accounts open on a dated reason and a reference, through the partners who already hold trust.
  • Hold the margin at the first scope. At 16.5%, Wipro's operating margin sits below its closest peers, so scope, pyramid and pricing model are set before a proposal goes in.

Operating margin, Q2 FY27

Wipro reports 16.5%, about seven and a half points below TCS. Each firm defines margin in its own way.

Strategy

Strategy

Grow share of wallet in Wipro's existing UK and Ireland accounts first (Move 1: expand), and use that proof to open new ones (Move 2: new logos).

Existing accounts qualify faster, convert at a higher rate and book order book sooner, and the references they produce make hunting credible. Both moves run from the first quarter. On screen: M3 Strategy and M4 Banking.

Move 1: expand

Existing accounts, from the first quarter

  1. Map IC&D white space in the top UK and Ireland accounts with the client partners, banking first.
  2. Attach QE, modernisation and AI to run contracts and renewals, where added scope offsets step-downs of 6% to 7% a year.
  3. Run executive workshops on dated forcing events that end in a proactive proposal.
  4. Phase first scopes so order book lands early and the next phase is already named.

Move 2: new logos

New accounts, hunting from the first quarter

  1. Take references from Move 1 to targets with a dated trigger: bank integrations, resilience, DORA and PS26/2.
  2. Build partner-sourced pipeline with Google, Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft and Tricentis.
  3. Open with a fixed-price diagnostic, then expand.
  4. Run hunting as a standing motion from the first quarter.

Why banking leads

The evidence is strongest there. Lloyds Banking Group is the largest named UK estate on the public record: a relationship of more than fifteen years, around 2,500 Wipro people, and two European Software Testing Awards in Lloyds' name, including the Payment Services Platform. UK banks also face fixed dates. FCA PS26/2 incident and third-party reporting applies from 18 March 2027, purpose codes become mandatory on every CHAPS payment in November 2027, and Parliament has been writing to banks and regulators about outages since 2025.

On the new-logo side the triggers are in the calendar. Coventry Building Society absorbs The Co-operative Bank on 1 January 2027, Santander and Nationwide have acquisitions to integrate, and the Google Cloud region failure of 20 October 2026 put cloud resilience on every bank board's agenda, with the regulators' full reply to the Treasury Committee due by 20 January 2027.

If the portfolio is another sector

The method holds and the dates change. Energy suppliers must complete migration to market-wide half-hourly settlement by 7 May 2027. The PSTN switch-off falls on 31 January 2027. Insurers and pension providers face Consumer Duty on closed books and the dashboards connection backstop. Public bodies publish pipeline notices under the Procurement Act each May, and Wipro is listed on G-Cloud 15. The chart below holds every dated event the research found, by sector.

Vision

What the practice looks like when this works

On screen: M6 Vision.

The IC&D practice in the UK and Ireland becomes the part of Wipro that client executives call before they write a tender. Each top account carries a standing IC&D point of view, refreshed against the client's regulatory calendar and strategy, and each dated event becomes a workshop that ends in a proposal. Quality engineering and modernisation enter through run contracts and renewals, AI enters through the data and adoption work those programmes expose, and the references compound into a new-logo motion that opens banks, insurers and utilities with proof. Order book arrives in phases, margin is set at the first scope, and the practice specialist is measured on both.
