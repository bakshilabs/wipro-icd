AI Workflow

I researched and built this site with Claude Code. Four research agents wrote sourced files, page agents wrote the pages to a shared style guide, and every page went through an independent review and automated browser checks. The second half of the page shows how I would bring the same working practices to a pursuit team.

Counts on this page come from the four research files and the build script behind this site, 27 October 2026.

What each agent brought back

Each claim in a research file carries a source, a retrieval date and a flag. Verified means a primary source such as a Wipro filing or a statute. Secondary means press, analyst or broker coverage. Unverified claims stay out of the pages unless they are labelled "to confirm".

Routines I would bring to pursuits
