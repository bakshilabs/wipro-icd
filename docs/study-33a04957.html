Who you are meeting

The interviewer

Raveendra Rayanna leads IC&D sales for Europe. He came up through delivery into banking sales, so he thinks in order book, margin and bank accounts.

What he will probe

  1. Numbers: order book, TCV, margin, pipeline coverage. Have your figures ready.
  2. Pursuit mechanics: qualification, working with client partners and pursuit teams, closing.
  3. Banking: how you would open and grow a UK bank account.
  4. Delivery credibility: how you keep what you sell deliverable and margin-positive.
  5. Partners: how you co-sell with hyperscalers and product partners.
  6. AI: real client value, data readiness, adoption.
  7. From your first interview: proactive against reactive deals, and contract types such as outcome-based and performance-based. Expect more depth.

How to use this page

  1. Read the fact sheets in Study materials, one tab a session.
  2. Work through Enterprise sales, starting with Deal origination and contracts.
  3. Say each model answer out loud with the timer. Keep to 60 or 90 seconds.
  4. Run the quiz by topic, then by job description line, then retry the ones you got wrong.
  5. Flip the flashcards until every number comes back without thinking.
  6. The night before, tick through the checklist and stop.

On the day: open on Accounts (A5) or Deals (A7) early, use banking examples first, and keep the language commercial. Refer to public Wipro material and leave personal details and LinkedIn out of it.
