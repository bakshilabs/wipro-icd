Workshops

This page shows how I would run executive workshops that align business, technology and customer experience priorities. It covers the formats I would draw on, a builder that returns an agenda for a client goal, audience and move, a sample output and the steps before and after that lead to a qualified opportunity. In my plan the executive workshop is the engine of Move 1, growing IC&D scope in Wipro's existing UKI accounts.

Formats from GV, Nielsen Norman Group, Gartner and Wardley mapping sources. Agendas, timings and use-case placements are my judgement, drawn from executive and board training I run at Atlas Learn.

Move 1: expand

Workshops in existing accounts

Move 1 grows share of wallet in accounts where Wipro already runs work. The executive workshop is how white space in an account becomes a qualified opportunity, and I would run each one with the client partner who owns the relationship.

  1. Map the white spaceWith the client partner, list what Wipro runs in the account, when contracts renew and which IC&D offerings (QE, modernisation, digital experience, AI) are not yet in place.
  2. Pick a dated forcing eventChoose one problem with a deadline the executive already owns, such as a regulatory date, a renewal or a cost target.
  3. Invite through the client partnerThe client partner asks the executive sponsor and agrees the goal with them, so the session sits inside the existing relationship.
  4. Run the workshop on live dataUse the run contract's own service data as the baseline, then score use cases and agree one to take forward.
  5. Turn the output into a proactive proposalSend a phased first scope within two weeks, attached to the run contract or renewal where it fits, and take it through the bid/no-bid scorecard.

What the client partner brings

Access to the sponsor, the account history and the renewal calendar. I bring the IC&D offer, the workshop design, Wipro's practice experts and the alliance partners.
