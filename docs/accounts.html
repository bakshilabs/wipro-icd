Accounts

This page sets out how I would choose which UK and Ireland accounts to hunt and which to grow, and how pipeline turns into IC&D order book and revenue. The accounts are illustrative archetypes built from public facts about each sector.

Archetype scores, weights and the offering adjacencies are my judgement. Pipeline figures and conversion rates are placeholders for Wipro's own data. Where a real company is named, the fact comes from a cited public source.

    Two motions

    Hunting and farming

    Hunting wins a first IC&D scope at a new logo. Farming grows an existing account from one offering to the next, through the Wipro client partner who owns it. The ranked leaders below follow the weights set above.

    Hunting new logos

    1. Pick accounts with a dated forcing event inside 18 months, so the first meeting has a reason.
    2. Open with an outside-in brief and an executive workshop on one problem, with Wipro's industry and alliance teams.
    3. Land a bounded first scope: an assessment, a test automation pack or one AI use case at a fixed price.
    4. Prove it in production within two quarters and agree the next scope at the review.

    Farming existing accounts

    1. Map what Wipro already runs in the account and who sponsors it, with the client partner.
    2. Find the next offering using the adjacency map below, starting from the one already delivered.
    3. Use the live service's own data as the business case for the next scope.
    4. Bring renewals forward into a wider deal where AI productivity can be shared through the price.
