# Banking, quality engineering and European regulation: research for the IC&D interview

Prepared for: Arpan Bakshi (London), application for "ICD Practitioner", Wipro Industry Cloud & Digital (IC&D), UK & Ireland. The next interviewer leads IC&D sales for Europe and has a background in banking, data and quality engineering.
Research date: 27 October 2026. All sources below were retrieved on **27 October 2026** unless stated otherwise.

**Flags used**

- **verified**: primary source (wipro.com, Wipro investor releases and transcripts, partner press releases, EU/ECB/EBA/ESA, Bank of England, FCA, HM Treasury, Central Bank of Ireland, Swift, EUR-Lex).
- **secondary**: press, analyst, law-firm or awards-organiser coverage.
- **unverified**: inference, or a single report not confirmed from a primary source.
- "(derived)" means my own inference or arithmetic on primary material.

Rules followed: the only Wipro individuals named are the CEO (Srini Pallia) and the Europe CEO (Omkar Nisal). Other Wipro executives quoted in releases are referred to by role only. No LinkedIn content was used. Client and partner organisations are named only where Wipro or the partner names them.
