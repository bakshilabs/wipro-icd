## 6. Interview angles (synthesis, not sourced claims)

- **IC&D is real and public.** Quote the 20-F wording: "enterprise transformation, application modernization, quality engineering, testing, and cloud-native platform services ... with our extensive partner and hyperscaler ecosystem."
- **Europe is the bright spot on margin.** Segment margin rose from about 12% to about 17% year on year, with modest CC growth (+0.6% QoQ), while group bookings were weak in Q2 FY27. Practitioners who help convert pipeline into large deals are therefore valuable.
- **The AI story is multi-model.** Gemini Enterprise is the orchestration layer (LIFT and Antigravity), Claude has an Applied AI CoE with a 10,000-certification target, Microsoft brings Copilot at scale, and all of it is packaged through WEGA (build) and WINGS (run). Expect questions on how you would use WEGA in a modernisation or QE bid.
- **Legacy modernisation is a stated demand theme for BFSI** (Q2 FY27 call). UK BFSI is Capco's home market, and the Phoenix ALPHA modernisation is the flagship UK reference.
