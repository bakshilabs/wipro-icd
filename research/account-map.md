# Wipro UK and Ireland account map: clients, white space and people (for the IC&D farming page)

Prepared for: Arpan Bakshi (London), application for "ICD Practitioner", Wipro Industry Cloud & Digital (IC&D), UK & Ireland.
Research date: 27 October 2026. All sources below were retrieved on **27 October 2026** unless stated otherwise.

**What this file adds.** It builds the account map behind the public "farming" page: Wipro's named UK and Irish clients, what Wipro does for each, where IC&D could add work, which Wipro teams and partners would be involved, and the named Wipro leaders who own the relevant parts of the business. It does not repeat `wipro.md`, `wipro-sales.md`, `bfs-europe.md`, `competitors.md` or `uki-market.md`. Where a fact is already there, this file cites it as, for example, "wipro.md #55", "bfs #73" or "uki C24" and adds only what is new.

**Flags used** (same as wipro.md)

- **verified**: primary source (wipro.com, Wipro filings, the client's or partner's own page or release, UK government contract records).
- **secondary**: reputable press, analyst notes, procurement aggregators that republish Find a Tender or eTenders notices, or a primary page read only through a search index.
- **unverified**: inference, a single unconfirmed report, or a page that could not be opened.
- **my reading**: my judgement, not a sourced claim.

**Public-page rules followed.** People are named only where Wipro, Capco, Designit, a Wipro partner or a reputable outlet names them publicly in a work context. No LinkedIn, ZoomInfo, ContactOut, RocketReach, The Org or other broker or social profile was used as a source, even where search results surfaced them. No contact details, home locations or personal information are recorded. Leadership profile pages on wipro.com include "based in" lines; these were deliberately left out.

**Business attribution.** "Delivering business" uses Wipro's current structure (Technology Services GBL = CISS, IC&D, Enterprise Applications (EA), Data, Analytics & AI (DAAI), Designit; plus BPS, Engineering, Consulting and Capco; see wipro.md #1, #11). Most sources predate the April 2025 structure, so the business column is **my mapping** of the scope described onto today's units, unless the source names the unit (for example "Wipro FullStride Cloud", IC&D's predecessor).
