Prepared for: ICD Practitioner interview preparation (Wipro, Industry Cloud & Digital, UKI)
Research date: 27 October 2026. All sources below were retrieved on 2026-10-27.

## How to read this file

Each claim carries a source URL, the retrieval date and a flag:

- **verified**: primary source (company website, regulatory filing, company press release, official procurement notice). Where marked **verified (extract)**, the primary URL was identified but the figure was read from a search-engine extract of that page rather than opened in full. Treat it as primary, but re-check the exact wording before quoting it.
- **secondary**: trade or financial press, analyst firms, brokers.
- **unverified**: aggregators, single-outlet reports, undated or possibly out-of-date figures, or vendor claims about outcomes.

Rules followed: no individuals named except CEOs; no LinkedIn content; financial years are as each company defines them (Indian firms' FY27 runs April 2026 to March 2027; Accenture's FY26 ended 31 August 2026; Kyndryl's FY26 ended 31 March 2026; Deloitte UK's FY26 ended 31 May 2026).

Note on coverage: the web-search allowance for this session ran out before every planned query had been run. Remaining gaps are listed at the end.

Synthesis (author's judgement, drawn from claims 86–95):

1. **Annuity work is being repriced.** AI productivity is being passed back to clients at renewal or earlier, typically worth 2–4% a year in traditional segments.
2. **Volume is compensating so far.** Vendors argue that more AI work and vendor consolidation make up the volume. Infosys and TCS report AI revenue above 10% of total (claims 21 and 32).
3. **New deals embed productivity commitments upfront** over 3–5 years, with gain-share or outcome pricing.
4. **Platform-plus-services pricing is emerging** (Globant AI Pods, Kyndryl "services-as-software", Wipro AI-native platforms), but it is still a small share of revenue.

For an ICD seller this means three things: lead with outcome commitments, show the productivity curve explicitly, and protect margin through platform IP rather than rate cards.
