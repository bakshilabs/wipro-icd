## 1. Demand heatmap (my judgement, not sourced data)

**Labelled judgement.** Scores run from 1 (weak demand) to 5 (strong, urgent, funded demand) for the next 12–24 months in the UK and Ireland. They are based on the evidence in sections 3–9.

| Sector | AI solutions | CX and digital experience | App / legacy modernisation | Quality engineering |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financial services (banks, insurers, pensions) | **5**: 75% of firms already use AI and the median number of use cases is expected to more than double; the FCA's Mills Review sets up agentic finance | **5**: Consumer Duty, 214,600 FOS complaints, APP fraud reimbursement and the motor finance redress all need better journeys | **5**: closed-book life and pensions books, a TSB migration, mainframes and the dashboards deadline | **5**: operational resilience (Mar 2025), DORA, 803 hours of bank outages and large migrations |
| Public sector and central government | **4**: the AI Action Plan (38 of 50 actions met) and £2bn for AI, but delivery is slow and buyers are cautious | **4**: 47% of services still non-digital; GOV.UK app and wallet | **5**: 28% of systems are legacy, 22% of those red-rated; HMRC/DWP mainframes; a £45bn/yr savings claim | **4**: Procurement Act and assurance needs; GDS service standard |
| Health (NHS, HSE Ireland) | **4**: AI diagnostics already cover a third of chest X-rays; AI in the NHS App | **4**: NHS App is the "front door"; 80m logins a month; satisfaction at 26% | **4**: single patient record, ESR replacement, NHSE–DHSC merger, Irish national EHR tender | **3**: clinical safety testing matters, but budgets are tight and much of the work is product-led |
| Retail and consumer | **4**: personalisation, agentic commerce, contact centre; adoption is broad but shallow | **5**: about 27% of sales are online; DMCC Act enforcement; retail lost the top UKCSI spot | **3**: modernisation is selective, with ERP/commerce replatforming | **3**: cyber recovery testing (M&S, Co-op) and peak-load testing |
| Utilities and energy (incl. water) | **3**: Ofgem ethical AI guidance and the AI Reg Lab; regulated pace | **4**: utilities are the lowest UKCSI sector (73.7); Energy Ombudsman cases up in H1 2026 | **4**: MHHS migration by May 2027; water regulator rebuild; smart data | **4**: MHHS testing and settlement cut-over to July 2027 |
| Telecoms and media | **4**: BT is shrinking its workforce through AI; contact-centre automation | **4**: mid-contract price rules; complaints at record lows, which must be kept there | **4**: PSTN switch-off on 31 Jan 2027; VodafoneThree integration; VMO2 IT outsourcing | **3**: the Telecoms Security Code (to 2028–29) drives assurance |
| Travel and transport | **3**: operations optimisation; AI adoption is slower | **4**: rail complaints run at 19 per 100,000 journeys; the GBR app; EES border friction | **4**: GBR merges 17 organisations into one; TfL cyber recovery | **3**: ticketing and border systems testing |

**Takeaways for the interview (judgement):**
1. Financial services is the strongest market and the closest fit to Wipro's existing UK work (Phoenix/ReAssure).
2. The public sector holds the largest legacy estate, but buying it goes through frameworks.
3. Utilities is the sector where CX is weakest, and it has hard MHHS dates.
