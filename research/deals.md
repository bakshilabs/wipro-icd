## 1. How IT services deals are priced and structured

### 1.1 Commercial models (what each one does to risk and margin)

| Model | How it is billed | Who carries delivery risk | What AI productivity does to it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time and materials (T&M) | Day or hour rate × effort used | Client | Efficiency gains go straight to the client as fewer billed hours |
| Fixed price / fixed scope | Price agreed upfront, revenue recognised by percentage of completion | Supplier (estimation risk) | Supplier keeps any saving it delivers below the estimate |
| Capacity / managed service | Monthly fee per FTE, pod, ticket band or service tower | Shared; supplier carries productivity risk | Clients ask for year-on-year productivity step-downs |
| Outcome-based | Price per transaction or outcome, or fee linked to a KPI | Supplier | The supplier earns the margin from automation |
| Gain-share | Base fee plus a share of the savings or revenue delivered | Shared | Needs agreed baselines and measurement |

## 5. Talking points for the interview (synthesis, not sourced claims)

1. **Margin-positive AI deals.** Wipro's IT services margin (16.5%) sits about 7.5pp below TCS (about 24%). Under T&M, each point of AI productivity is lost revenue. Moving the right scopes to fixed-price, managed-capacity-with-productivity-glidepath or outcome pricing keeps part of the gain (claims 1–6, 21–26).
2. **Estimate AI productivity by task type, not as a blanket discount.** The evidence ranges from −19% (METR) to +56% (Copilot RCT). Use task-level factors as explicit assumptions and tie them to contingency (claims 30–35).
3. **Qualify harder.** Global win rates fell to 39%, and top performers use go/no-go gates (81%). MEDDPICC "Metrics" become the outcome KPIs, and Procurement Act s.52 makes those KPIs public above £5m (claims 38–53).
4. **CX engagement model.** Use AI to augment agents first (NBER +14%/+34%) and automate where it is safe (Fin 51% average resolution). Plan for a human in the loop (Klarna reversal; Gartner says half of planned workforce cuts will be abandoned) and design to FCA Consumer Duty, ICO ADM and EU AI Act Article 50 duties (claims 62–79).
5. **Workshop sequence for an executive client.** Run journey map → service blueprint → AI use-case prioritisation (value × feasibility) → Wardley map (build/buy/partner) → a 5-day design sprint on the top use case. The output feeds the solution and the price.
