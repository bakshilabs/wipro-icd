# New logos: target universe, triggers, public sector pipeline, routes in and entry offers (UK and Ireland)

Prepared for: Arpan Bakshi, application for "ICD Practitioner", Wipro Industry Cloud & Digital (IC&D), UK & Ireland. Supports the website page "New Logos" (Move 2: use proof from expanding existing accounts to open new ones, banking first, then insurers, utilities and the public sector).
Research date: 27 October 2026. All sources were retrieved on **27 October 2026** unless stated otherwise.

**Flags used**

- **verified**: primary source (the organisation's own results, annual report, press release or website; regulator, Parliament, GOV.UK, Find a Tender, Government Commercial Agency; a vendor's own programme page).
- **secondary**: press, trade press, analyst, law-firm or supplier case-study coverage.
- **unverified**: single weak source, search-extract only, aggregator, or figures that conflict.

**Rules followed**

- Facts only. This file does not say whether any organisation is or is not a Wipro client. Wipro's named UKI clients are covered separately in `account-map.md`.
- No individuals are named, except chief executives where a source names them. Where an organisation announced a new CIO, CTO or COO, only the organisation and role are given.
- No LinkedIn or data-broker content. Several search hits were LinkedIn posts (for example a building society's interim CIO and a bank's divisional CIO). They were excluded.
- To avoid repetition, this file only cross-refers to facts already in `uki-market.md` (C-numbers), `deals.md`, `sales-scope.md`, `wipro-sales.md`, `bfs-europe.md` and `competitors.md`.
