## What this means for a practice specialist (my reading)

- **"Own contribution to IC&D Revenue and Order Book (OB) targets."** Wipro's client partners are measured on "revenue, bookings and OM" (claim 86). Expect your number to be practice OB credited inside SMU accounts. Know the Q2 FY27 position: US$674m of large deals, slipped signatures, and the "M1, M2 rather than M3" closing discipline (claims 63, 65). Show you forecast honestly and close early in the quarter.
- **"Partner with Account Managers, Client Partners, and Pursuit Teams."** The structure is SMU-led (claim 1), with GAEs and client partners owning accounts and dedicated pursuit teams on large bids (claims 3–4, 47, 88). Position yourself as the IC&D offer owner who makes the client partner's account plan bigger, not as a parallel seller.
- **"Drive hunting and farming motions."** Ninety-eight per cent of revenue comes from existing clients and the active-client count is falling (claims 24–26). Farming metal and strategic accounts is the main engine, yet "hunting remains a very, very key lever" (claim 11). Prepare one farming story (expanding into an existing UK BFSI estate) and one hunting story (a new logo in a priority sector).
- **"Shape commercially viable and margin-positive deals."** Management openly says consolidation deals carry price pressure and upfront investment, while "net new reimagine AI" work has "much better" margins (claims 59–61, 64). Lead with modernisation scopes that use WEGA and WINGS productivity. Price in outcome, consumption or hybrid models (claim 84), and model token cost and TCO (claim 64).
- **"Act as Practice Specialist for IC&D pursuits, supporting opportunity qualification."** Pallia ties large-deal wins to proactive shaping and to "picking the right deal" (claim 44). Use the MEDDPICC and bid-gate material in deals.md to show how you would qualify out early.
- **"Position and sell ... Application and Cloud Modernisation, APR, Legacy Modernisation."** The Q2 FY27 BFSI demand list is almost the IC&D catalogue: legacy and core modernisation, cloud migration and "application rationalization" (claim 76). Wipro's 2026 Google Cloud award is specifically for application modernisation (claim 92). Use both as proof points.
- **"AI Solutions – Gemini/Claude" and "AI-enabled business transformation."** The AI-Native unit with 2,000+ FDEs is winning "outcome-based deals" (claim 78), and sales now pitch "platform plus service" (claim 79). Be ready to explain how an IC&D modernisation bid hands off to, or bundles, an AI-native platform or SLM.
- **"Expand Wipro's presence across existing and new strategic UKI clients."** UKI was a P&L of over US$1bn when its head became Europe CEO (claims 31–32). Management has repeatedly named the UK and BFSI as Europe's bright spots (claims 72–74), and GBP billing has risen to 13% (claim 99). The UK is where Europe's turnaround is being proven.
- **"Collaborate with ... alliance partners."** Co-sell assets exist: 100+ Microsoft co-sell-ready Marketplace offers, AWS Marketplace listings, an AWS collaboration award and a Google Cloud application-modernisation award (claims 90–93). Name one partner-sourced pipeline idea for a UKI account.
- **"Facilitating executive workshops."** Consulting and Capco originate deals at C-suite level, and Pallia credits consulting with "originating larger opportunities" (claims 5–6, 77). Offer to run joint workshops with Wipro Consulting or Capco rather than presenting IC&D in isolation.

## Gaps and to confirm

1. **Deal-review governance:** Wipro's internal deal-qualification and approval gates (thresholds, sign-off levels, any named framework) are not public. The only evidence is job-board text (claims 45–47). Ask in interview how IC&D pursuits are reviewed and who signs off pricing.
2. **Mega-deal definition:** the US$500m+ threshold comes from a broker (claim 39), not Wipro. Wipro has not disclosed TCVs for the four FY26 mega deals beyond Phoenix (£500m) and Olam (>US$1bn).
3. **Olam booking quarter:** it is unclear whether Olam was booked in Q4 FY26 or Q1 FY27 (announced 6 Apr 2026, after the Q4 period end).
4. **"Metal accounts" tiers:** the public deck used "Metal / Future Metal / Big Circle" up to FY26 but dropped the term in the Q2 FY27 deck. The tier names (platinum, gold and so on) and their thresholds are not published. Do not quote tier names as fact.
5. **SMU share restatement:** Europe's FY25 share (27.1%) and FY26 share (26.5%) are on different SMU definitions (LatAm and Canada moved in April 2026; see wipro.md #8). Compare like-for-like only within one datasheet.
6. **UKI-specific numbers:** UKI revenue, client counts and IC&D's share of UKI order book are not disclosed. The "over US$1bn" UKI P&L dates from November 2024.
7. **Practice-specialist operating model:** how OB credit is split between the SMU account team and the GBL practice is not public. Confirm with the recruiter or hiring manager.
8. **Job postings:** the IC&D Practice Specialist text came from a cached copy (the live page is closed). The UK Account Executive, Transformation Partner and Client Engagement Partner texts come from job boards. Several other postings were read only through a search index.
9. **ServiceNow tier:** wipro.com says "Elite Global Partner", but ServiceNow's partner finder shows "Elite". Use "Elite partner" in interview.
10. **Q3 FY27:** results (due mid-January 2027) will show whether the two slipped Q2 deals signed. Re-check before any later interview round.
