Shared by the agents that thread the strategy through the site. Read agents/page-brief.md and agents/review-brief.md first.

## The strategy
Grow share of wallet in Wipro's existing UKI accounts first, and use that proof to open new ones.

Why this order, from Wipro's own figures (research/wipro-sales.md, bfs-europe.md; cite claim sources on the page):
- 98% of Wipro revenue comes from existing clients.
- Clients above US$100m went from 22 to 16 and are now 18, so rebuilding large accounts is the fastest order book lever.
- Europe growth in Q2 FY27 was led by BFSI, where Wipro has relationships, QE awards and partners.

Move 1, expand: map IC&D white space in top UKI accounts with client partners; attach QE, modernisation and AI to run contracts and renewals; executive workshops on dated forcing events produce proactive proposals; phased first scopes so order book lands early.

Move 2, convert proof into new logos: references from expansions; forcing-event-led hunting, banking first (operational resilience, ISO 20022, DORA); partner-sourced pipeline (Tricentis, AWS, Google, Anthropic). Hunting runs from the first quarter. The CFO's line "hunting remains a very, very key lever" supports this move (Aparna Iyer, CFO, may be named).

Measures: share of wallet per account, pipeline created and coverage, win rate, order book booked.

Proof that I can run it (only where a page talks about my experience, short): the motion I ran at Foster + Partners, adding consulting and engineering scope to design clients through the partner who held the relationship; at WSP and SOM I won new clients through outreach, pitches and bids.
